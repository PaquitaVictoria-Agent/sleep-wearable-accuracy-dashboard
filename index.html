Read this first

Not all numbers mean the same thing. “PSG agreement” is not directly interchangeable with sensitivity/specificity or sleep-stage kappa. I show the metric source and confidence so we do not pretend precision where the literature is messy.

Best use: compare price-to-evidence, then click a device for caveats.

Method note: prices are current online prices I observed during this session where possible. Accuracy data is a normalized buyer-facing view from CircaTest pages and the peer-reviewed studies they link, especially Chinoy 2021, Haghayegh 2019, Concheiro-Moscoso 2023, Lee 2025 meta-analysis, and device-specific CircaTest summaries. Unknown values are deliberately left blank rather than filled with fake confidence.
